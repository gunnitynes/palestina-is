# palestina.is: Félagið Ísland-Palestína

Static rebuild (v2.0) of the website of Félagið Ísland-Palestína, the Iceland–Palestine Association.

The original WordPress site went offline when its hosting provider's servers were shut down.
Its pages were recovered from the Internet Archive's Wayback Machine and rebuilt here as a plain,
self-contained static site: the same texts and the same web addresses, with no database, no tracking
and no third-party scripts.

- News (Fréttir), articles (Greinar), the association's pages, photo galleries and older archive pages
- The membership and contact forms open the visitor's own email program, addressed to palestina@palestina.is
- Fonts and images are self-hosted
